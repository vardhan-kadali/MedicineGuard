Verifiable Medicine Batch Traceability

MedicineGuard is a medicine batch traceability and digital record verification system that uses SHA-256 hashing and Merkle Trees.

Role-Based Experiences

Manufacturer

Register and manage medicine batches. Generates canonical MG01 records, builds balanced Merkle Trees, and produces individual unit QR references.

Open Portal

Pharmacist

Inspect and verify medicine records. Evaluates MGP1 cryptographic proofs against manufacturer root commitments with complete path transparency.

Open Verification

Customer

Check a medicine's digital record against an authorized batch root. Demonstrates client-side Merkle proof verification for pharmaceutical packages.

Cryptographic Traceability Lifecycle
1
Batch Registration

Manufacturer compiles individual medicine units with unique identifiers into canonical MG01 binary structures.

2
Root Commitment

The C/WASM engine constructs a Merkle Tree, generating a single 32-byte cryptographic root committing all units.

3
Unit QR References

Each package receives an encoded QR reference with its unit serial, batch root, and compact MGP1 sibling path.

4
Instant Verification

Pharmacist or customer inspects the proof in O(log N) time without requiring a centralized record database.

Algorithmic Invariants & Security Guarantees

MedicineGuard implements strict domain separation (RFC 6962) with a 0x00 prefix on leaf nodes and 0x01 on internal parents to mathematically rule out second-preimage collision attacks. Odd nodes are promoted deterministically without duplicating data, preventing Bitcoin-style tree duplication vulnerabilities.
